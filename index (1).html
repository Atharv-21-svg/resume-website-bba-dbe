01 ABOUT

Learning by doing.

Coming from Nashik, I didn’t have a direct route into the startup world. I found myself drawn to it through curiosity, conversations and a willingness to try things before I had everything figured out.

Meeting ambitious peers through The Knowledge Society (TKS) broadened my sense of what I could pursue. It encouraged me to take my interests more seriously and learn by building, not just observing.

That curiosity has led me to explore startup research, AI-native workflows and venture capital through independent projects. I’m still early in the journey, so I’m focused on developing sound judgment, understanding how businesses work and contributing work that is genuinely useful.
